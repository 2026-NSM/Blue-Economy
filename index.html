About the Symposium

Where research, policy, innovation and action converge.

Vembanad Dialogues 2026 is a Global Symposium on Keralam’s Blue Economy, bringing together researchers, policymakers, government representatives, industry leaders, entrepreneurs, development agencies, students, professionals and community stakeholders to explore the opportunities and challenges shaping a sustainable blue future.

Hosted by Naipunnya School of Management, Cherthala, the two-day symposium is anchored in the emerging vision of positioning Alappuzha as India’s Blue Economy Capital. It connects research evidence with policy priorities, entrepreneurial opportunities, community needs and practical development pathways.

“Positioning Alappuzha as India’s Blue Economy Capital.”Research · Policy · Innovation · Enterprise · Sustainability · Community

About Naipunnya

Where professionalism meets purpose.

As its name signifies, Naipunnya aims to foster professionalism, discipline, and the holistic development of its students.

Established 2003

Naipunnya School of Management, Cherthala, takes a unique approach to professional education, providing a platform for every individual to refine and mould their talents.

Established in 2003, Naipunnya School of Management, Cherthala, is a project of the Major Archeparchy of Ernakulam-Angamaly. His Beatitude Mar Raphael Thattil, the Major Archbishop of Ernakulam-Angamaly, serves as the Chief Patron, while Mar Joseph Pamplany, the Vicar of the Major Archbishop, is the Co-Patron.

Affiliated with the University of Kerala, Naipunnya has emerged as a byword for academic and professional excellence. The institution is accredited with an 'A' grade by NAAC, approved by AICTE, and recognized under Section 2(f) of the UGC Act of 1956.

Over the years, Naipunnya has earned numerous national and international awards and accolades.
