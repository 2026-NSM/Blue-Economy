About the Symposium

Where research, policy, innovation and action converge.

Vembanad Dialogues 2026 is a Global Symposium on Keralam’s Blue Economy, bringing together scholars, policymakers, industry leaders, government representatives and community stakeholders to explore opportunities for sustainable, inclusive and resilient blue growth.

Anchored in the vision of positioning Alappuzha as India’s Blue Economy Capital, the symposium connects research, policy, enterprise, innovation and community perspectives to shape meaningful pathways for Kerala’s blue future.

“Positioning Alappuzha as India’s Blue Economy Capital.”Research · Policy · Innovation · Enterprise · Sustainability · Community

The Blue Economy offers a strategic pathway for sustainable use of ocean, coastal and inland water resources while advancing livelihoods, food security, climate resilience and inclusive economic growth. With its coastline, Vembanad Lake, inland waterways, fisheries, aquaculture, coir heritage and globally renowned backwater tourism, Alappuzha is uniquely positioned to contribute to this emerging blue future.

The symposium is conceived as an International Collaboration Programme, bringing together perspectives from different nations and participation from representatives of various Ministries and Departments of the Government of Kerala.

Thematic Research Tracks

Seven pathways into the Blue Economy.

The seven themes below are the primary focus of the symposium. Chairs and co-chairs are part of the programme structure and are intentionally not repeated within the theme descriptions.

THEME 01

Fisheries, Aquaculture & Coastal Livelihoods

Sub-themes: Sustainable Marine Fishing, Advanced Inland Fisheries, Mariculture Innovation, Seaweed Cultivation, Coastal Livelihood Diversification, Women Entrepreneurs in Fisheries, Fisher Welfare and Safety.

THEME 02

Blue Tourism, Recreation & Cultural Heritage

Sub-themes: Sustainable Beach & Backwater Tourism, Marine Cruise & Wellness Tourism, Eco and Cultural Tourism, Maritime Heritage and Ancient Trade, Maritime Museums, Coastal History.

THEME 03

Marine Industries & Blue Biotechnology

Sub-themes: Advanced Seafood Processing, Value-added Marine Products, Marine Biotechnology Applications, Blue Pharmaceuticals, Sea-based Nutraceuticals, Bioactive Marine Compounds.

THEME 04

Climate Action, Conservation & Renewable Energy

Sub-themes: Mangrove Restoration, Wetlands Protection, Marine Biodiversity, Blue Carbon Markets, Offshore Wind & Wave Energy, Floating Solar Power, Marine Plastic Recycling.

THEME 05

Education, Skills, Communication & Digital Blue Economy

Sub-themes: Blue Economy English, English as Economic Infrastructure, Maritime Communication, Specialised Maritime Education, Blue Entrepreneurship Training, Marine GIS & Remote Sensing, Oceanographic Sciences, AI applications in Fisheries, AI for Blue economy, IT for conservation and sustainability, Digital Humanities.

THEME 06

Coastal Agriculture & Blue Cuisine

Sub-themes: Integrated Coastal Agriculture, Saline-resistant Farming, Commercial Aquaponics, Coastal Ethnic Culinary Traditions, Sustainable Blue Cuisine.

THEME 07

Blue Finance & Emerging Policy Perspectives

Sub-themes: Sustainable Blue Investments, Blue Bonds & ESG Financing, Interdisciplinary Policy Research, Regional Policy Dialogue, Blue Innovation Ecosystems, Coastal Resilience Policy.

Open & Interdisciplinary Submissions: While the above themes are our primary focus, we welcome submissions on related topics that align with the conference's overall objectives. We encourage innovative, interdisciplinary research that advances sustainable Blue Economy knowledge and practice.
